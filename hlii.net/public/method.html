Signal stack

No single metric drives the board. Cash is one part of a wider market view.

1

Earnings quality

Revenue shape, margin quality, and whether the quarter looks strong beyond headline EPS.

2

Cash resilience

Operating cash flow, conversion, financing pressure, and balance-sheet slack without overclaiming hidden cash state.

3

Direction of travel

Whether the operating path is improving, stalling, or degrading across recent quarters.

4

Price momentum

How price is behaving around the quarter, relative trend, and whether the stock is already running ahead.

5

Relative volume

Whether moves are happening on conviction or thin participation.

6

Price alignment

A rough read on whether market action matches what the operating score suggests.
