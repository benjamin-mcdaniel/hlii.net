Method

How HLII v1 works

1. Cash pressure

HLII asks whether the quarter generated cash cleanly or relied on balance-sheet slack. Signals include operating cash flow trend, free cash flow conversion, interest burden, and working-capital strain.

2. Scaling friction

Growth that requires rising overhead, repeated restructuring, or heavy capital intensity is treated as fragile scale. HLII rewards cleaner operating leverage and punishes recurring friction.

3. Direction of travel

The score is less about one good print than whether revenue quality, margin shape, and management posture are improving or degrading across consecutive quarters.

V1 scope: large public companies, quarterly cadence, filings-first interpretation.

Planned expansions include macro overlays for cost of money, commodity exposure, and price-versus-fundamental divergence once the core scoring is stable.
