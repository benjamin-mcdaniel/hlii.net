Overview Reports Method

Method

How we count.

HLII is methodology-first. We prefer fewer, cleaner counts to many noisy ones. Every report names the dataset, the window, the filter, and the source.

Bills
Source & scope

Bills are sourced from public legislative trackers. Each bill is tagged by topic, sponsor party, and stage reached (introduced, reported, passed chamber, signed). Reports state the chamber and window used.

Rulings
Source & scope

Rulings are sourced from court opinion feeds. Each is tagged by outcome (affirm, reverse, remand) and broad subject. We do not opine on correctness — we count.

Comparison
Windows

"Last 2 years" and "prior 2 years" comparisons use rolling windows ending at the report's publish date. Where a structural condition matters (e.g. 50/50 split), the window matches the duration of that condition.

Publishing
PDF-first

Reports are published as PDFs so the numbers, charts, and dataset references stay fixed at the moment of publication. The site catalog adds new reports as they ship.

Caveats

Things we are explicit about

Bill counts include re-introductions; reports note this when material.
Rulings counts exclude per curiam orders unless explicitly included.
State-level data lags federal; state-only reports name the source jurisdictions.
No predictions. HLII reports describe what happened, not what will happen.
